Dog Ear Books can be found a block off Main Street in Downtown Russellville. They mostly focus on a well curated collection of new books for all ages, with a small growing selection of used. As a bonus you can find Retro Roasts coffee shop in the back with a good amount of space to sit and read your latest find.

They also have local events with authors and a variety of book clubs.
